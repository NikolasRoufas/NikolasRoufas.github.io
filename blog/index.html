    Can Transformers Reliably Anonymize Legal Documents?

    Adapting BERT to legal language lifted entity-level F1 from 93.6% for the best legal-domain baseline to 99.4% on held-out EU judgments. This note follows the experiments behind that number, and explains why a 99% F1 score still does not make redaction reliable on its own.

    Based on Karamitsos, Roufas, Al-Hussaeni & Kanavos, Frontiers in Artificial Intelligence, 2025
